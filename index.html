Key Insights

  • Indonesia leads. 1,055 active users in Q2 2025, ahead of the US (815) — about 57% of the 1,848 distinct Q2 users, and 29% of all events (2,936).
  • Audience is flat. Total MAU held between 1,129 and 1,170 in every full month from April to August — stable, not growing.
  • Referral is the only clear riser. Referral MAU rose +19.7% from April to August (244 → 292, with a July dip to 255), while social fell −13.0% (276 → 240).
  • Mobile first. 89.6% of sessions are Android or iOS; desktop is only 10.4%.
  • Search converts late. Only 0.13% of searching users (1 of 750) added to cart the same day they searched — search intent pays off on later days, so attribution windows matter.
  • Vietnam is shrinking. VN MAU fell −23.7% from April to August (173 → 132), the steepest country decline; the US is down 9.3% (332 → 301).

Data Notes & Caveats

  • Every session holds exactly one event (10,000 sessions = 10,000 events), so session depth and duration cannot be analysed.
  • Users appear in more than one country and channel, so country and channel figures overlap: the April channel MAU values sum to 1,570 against 1,142 total users.
  • Purchase events (509) exceed checkout events (503), so a few purchases have no recorded checkout; at user level the gap is 453 vs 449.
  • March (27–31 Mar, 239 events) and September (to 23 Sep, 1,270 events) are partial months; compare full months only.
  • Total MAU, the Q2 total of 1,848 and the search figures (750 users, 1 same-day) come from the source analysis and cannot be recomputed from the aggregates in this file.
